How it works

Terrain: USGS/JPL Mars 2020 CTX DEM of Jezero (20 m/px), resampled here to 80 m. A* search over a 16-neighbour grid. Each step costs walking time (Tobler, slowed for a pressurised suit) + metabolic work (Minetti gradient model, Mars gravity) + risk (slope, steep descents, side-slope, roughness, closeness to cliffs). Slopes above the limit, cliffs and bad data are no-go. Rest stops are picked on flat, hazard-free ground every ~45 min.

The Python version plans at the full 20 m resolution. Boulders smaller than the DEM resolution aren't visible to it, so check final routes against HiRISE imagery.
